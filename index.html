How it works

1

One kibble at a time

An auger dispenser releases a single piece of kibble from the sealed hopper on top.

2

Rotating chute

A rotating chute sends the kibble to one of the six burrows around the base.

3

Solve the burrow

Your dog opens that burrow's lid — nose-push flap, paw-slide, lift-and-tilt, or pull-rope.

4

IR sensors track progress

Each burrow's IR sensor logs how fast and how often your dog solves it.

Six burrows, six mechanisms

Every burrow uses a different lid so your dog never memorises one solution.

Nose-push flap

A soft flap your dog pushes open with its nose.

Paw-slide

A sliding panel nudged aside with a paw.

Lift-and-tilt

A lid that lifts and tilts to reveal the kibble.

Pull-rope

A rope your dog tugs to release the lid.

Twist-lock

A rotating lid your dog turns to align the opening.

Press-and-hold

A button your dog holds down to dispense the treat.

Adaptive difficulty

Huntly adapts to your dog — no two hunts are the same.

Solving too fast?

Huntly tightens the latches, lengthens gaps between drops, and rotates which burrow gets food.

Losing interest?

Huntly loosens the latches and drops a more-open "hint" reward to re-engage your dog.

Stretches one ration across hours

A daily ration cap prevents overfeeding — one day's kibble lasts 2–4 hours of hunting.

Built for safety

Daily ration cap
